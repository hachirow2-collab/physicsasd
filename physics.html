How to use:
Drag the listener or ruler on the canvas. Change frequency, amplitude, and wave speed using the sliders. Turn on sound to hear the changes.

Physics note: Sound is a longitudinal wave made of compressions and rarefactions. The alternating bands are a visual model of wave fronts. Wavelength is calculated using λ = v ÷ f, and period using T = 1 ÷ f. The audio is a simplified tone model, not a full simulation of sound traveling through air.
